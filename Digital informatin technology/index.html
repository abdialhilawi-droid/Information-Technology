ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Learning technology through practice.

The Department of Information Technology prepares learners to understand, design and manage modern digital systems. Our approach combines classroom knowledge with practical activities that encourage problem-solving, creativity and responsible use of technology.

Students gain a foundation for careers in software development, information systems, networking, database administration and cybersecurity.
